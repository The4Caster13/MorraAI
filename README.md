Morra AI

The number one issue we ran into as IB students was a lack of proper resources to practice IOs (Individual Orals). These projects made up 30-35% of our French grade and our teacher Mrs. Morra graded us so horrendously we decided to build a project that made sure that never happens to any more poor souls. This was our goal behind Morra AI. The AI gives a random prompt then analyzes your speech, content and delivery, to provide perfect feedback similar to the real IB exam environment.
